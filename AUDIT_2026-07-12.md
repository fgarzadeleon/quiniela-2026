# Scoring Audit — 2026-07-12 (post-Quarter-Finals)

Full audit of every player's `total_points` against actual match results and the
scoring rules, run after the Quarter-Finals concluded (Semi-Finals not yet played).

## Method

1. **Independent team-level ledger.** Rebuilt every team's match-by-match results and
   round-advance attribution from raw `football-data.org` data in a standalone script
   (not reusing any app code), applying the rules directly:
   - Knockout W/D/L and goal points are based on the **AET score** (penalty-shootout
     goals excluded).
   - A knockout draw decided on penalties scores as a **draw** for both teams' match
     points, but the **Round Advanced bonus goes only to the shootout winner**.
   - Group-stage advance bonus awarded once per qualifying team, attributed to the
     matchday it was mathematically clinched (early: 6pts from 2 games; otherwise MD3).
   - Each knockout round won earns one Round Advanced bonus in that round's own column;
     winning the Final earns the Champion bonus instead of another Round Advanced bonus.
   - Diffed against production's `/api/audit-matches` for all **48 teams** — **0 diffs**.

2. **Independent per-player total.** Applied the wildcard-split logic
   (`wildcard_effective_from` + per-slot old/new team lists, aggregated **by team name,
   not slot** — a team present anywhere in both the old and new lineup keeps its
   full-tournament total under one label; a team new to the roster only earns from the
   wildcard-effective stage onward) to the independently-computed team ledger, and
   diffed the result against production's `/api/ranking` for all **30 players**
   (`total_points`, `team_points`, `old_team_points`) — **0 diffs**.

3. **Parallel per-player review.** Spawned one agent per player to manually recompute
   their score from the raw match data and rulebook, blind to the numeric cross-check
   above, and flag anything that looked wrong.

## Note on the parallel-review pass

8 of the 30 per-player agents initially flagged apparent discrepancies. Every one of
them traced back to an ambiguity in the audit instructions given to the agents, not an
actual bug — confirmed by re-checking against the independent numeric ledger above:

- **Team aggregation is by name, not slot.** A team present anywhere in both the old and
  new lineup (even under a different slot number) keeps its full-tournament total under
  one label — some agents reasoned per-slot and flagged this as mis-attribution
  (`ErickEsquer`, `Joe Martin`, `Peti`, `Jay`).
- **A team new to the roster only scores from the wildcard-effective stage onward** —
  it does not retroactively get full-tournament credit just because its name is in the
  current lineup. Two agents assumed the opposite (`Face`, `FedeFiesta`).
- **`old_team_points` is correctly included in the grand total** — it's not a
  display-only artifact; a player keeps points earned by a team before it was
  wildcarded away. Two agents assumed it should be excluded (`Esquer`, `Hoolie`).

No corrections were made to the scoring code — the site was correct in all cases.
